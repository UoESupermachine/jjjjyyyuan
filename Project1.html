SuperWind | HTS Wind Generator

Air‑core HTS coil arrays with modular cryogenics for multi‑MW direct‑drive offshore wind and high power‑density machines.

Explore the project

Programme

  • Programme: RAEng Chair in Emerging Technology — SuperMachine (NetZero energy & transport).

Lead

  • Lead: Professor Markus Mueller, University of Edinburgh.

Platforms & Targets

  • Platforms: SuperWind, SuperAero, SuperMarine. Targets: Y1–5 de-risking and demonstrator at TRL3/4; Y6–10+ towards industrial demonstration at TRL5/6.

Themes

Theme A

Theme B

Theme D

Platform-level demonstrators & industry collaboration.

Context & Need

Offshore wind targets

UK: 40 GW by 2030; ~116 GW by 2050, with 4–5 GW per year. Turbines trending towards 15‑MW class.

Generators — reference points

IEA 15‑MW reference: PM generator ~370 t, ~40 W/kg. EcoSwing HTS generator: 3.6 MW, ~68.8 t, ~52 W/kg.

SuperMachine concept

Ironless structural support; HTS coil arrays replacing PMs and copper coils; brushless flux‑pump excitation; aiming for higher fields/current density and reduced reliance on rare earths.

Themes & Y1–Y5 focus

  • Use the 1-MW C-GEN as the electromagnetic baseline (2D/3D FEA & Biot–Savart models), develop sizing tools and mechanical assessment methods.
  • Comparative designs and small demonstrators for experimental validation; include AC-loss modelling and exploratory ML methods.
  • Inspired by EU SUPRAPOWER (KIT): modular, pair-per-pole, layered and replaceable cryogenic cavity concept (A1–A5 with end windings).
  • Design for maintainability and replaceability; early component design and prototype validation.
  • Brushless excitation of HTS coils using rotating/linear/standing-wave flux pumps; establish an open-circuit voltage (Voc) design tool.
  • Outputs & dissemination: EUCAS 2023, IET PEMD 2023, EFATS 2023, etc.
  • Platform-level demonstrators for SuperWind / SuperAero / SuperMarine with industry collaboration and matched funding (indicative single project £5m+).
  • Trajectory: TRL3/4 → TRL5/6; UK supply-chain collaboration and commercial pathways (IP/incubation/licensing).
